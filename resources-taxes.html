Important deadlines

Mark these on the calendar.

Jan 31
W-2 & 1099 Distribution Deadline Employers must send W-2 forms to employees and 1099 forms to contractors by this date. Also the deadline to file these forms with the IRS/SSA in most cases.
Mar 15
Partnership & S-Corp Returns Due Form 1065 (Partnerships) and Form 1120-S (S-Corporations) must be filed or extensions requested.
Apr 15
Individual & C-Corp Filing Deadline Individual income tax returns (Form 1040) and C-Corporation returns (Form 1120) are due — unless extended. Q1 estimated payments also due.
Jun 15
Q2 Estimated Tax Payments Second quarter estimated tax payments due for individuals and businesses making quarterly payments.
Sep 15
Q3 Estimates & Partnership/S-Corp Extensions Third quarter estimated payments due. Extended partnership and S-Corp returns must be filed.
Oct 15
Extended Individual Returns Due Final deadline for individuals who filed an extension in April. C-Corp extended returns also due.
Jan 15
Q4 Estimated Tax Payments Fourth quarter estimated payments for the prior tax year. Closing the year cleanly so April isn't a surprise.
