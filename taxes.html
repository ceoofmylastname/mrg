What we handle

✦ Personal Taxes

For individuals who want to keep more of what they earn.

From simple W-2 filings to complex returns involving real estate, investments, K-1s, and high-earner deductions — we treat your personal return with the same strategic care we give our business clients.

  • Individual Tax Preparation Precise, compliant filings designed to deliver the best legitimate outcome possible — not the fastest one.
  • Tax Credits & Deductions We make sure you don't leave money on the table. Every credit, every deduction, every opportunity reviewed.
  • Amended & Prior Year Returns Mistakes on past returns or missed years? We'll correct, file, and recover what you're owed.
  • Tax Planning Year-round strategies to minimize taxes and prepare for the future. Real planning beats reactive filing every time.
✦ Business Tax Services

For business owners who want strategy, not surprises.

From sole proprietorships to multi-entity structures, we handle the full scope of small business tax — filings, planning, sales tax, and the strategic entity guidance that quietly saves thousands every year.

  • Small Business Tax Preparation Tailored solutions for sole proprietors, LLCs, S-Corps, C-Corps, and partnerships — handled by someone who actually knows your business.
  • Quarterly & Annual Filings Stay on top of every IRS and state requirement. Estimated payments, quarterly forms, year-end filings — never miss a deadline.
  • Sales Tax Reporting Accurate, timely submissions across the states you operate in — avoiding penalties and audit triggers.
  • Entity Selection & Tax Strategy Guidance on choosing the right structure for tax efficiency — and restructuring when growth demands it.
