The story

Why I built MRG.

i.   I spent over two decades inside corporate finance. Big budgets, complex models, strategic forecasts — the kind of high-stakes financial work most people never see. I learned the discipline of the field at the highest levels.

ii.   But I also watched something happen, year after year. Small business owners — the ones with the courage to bet on themselves — were getting the worst version of financial services. Reactive tax prep. Generic advice. Faceless firms that saw them as line items, not people.

iii.   So in 2018, I left Corporate America to build the opposite. A firm grounded in real relationships, real strategy, and the kind of care you'd expect from someone who actually wants you to succeed.

"I left Corporate America to build a firm where small business owners are treated like the priority they actually are — not the afterthought they've been made to feel like."

iv.   MRG Accounting Service offers more than bookkeeping and tax preparation. I provide CFO-level advisory support — giving small business owners the clarity and insights they need to make confident financial decisions. The kind of decisions that compound into real growth over time.

v.   My philosophy is simple. When you partner with me, you gain more than an accountant. You gain a trusted ally committed to your success.

The principles

Three values. No exceptions.

i.

Integrity

Plain answers, honest pricing, real conversations. If something isn't the right fit, I'll tell you. If we can save you money, we will. Nothing hidden, nothing inflated.

ii.

Dedication

Year-round commitment, not seasonal scrambling. Your books are watched monthly, your taxes are planned all year, and your strategic decisions get the attention they deserve.

iii.

Personalized Care

No two businesses are alike, so no two engagements are either. You get a partner who knows your specific business, your specific goals, and your specific challenges.
