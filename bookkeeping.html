What we offer

Every piece of the bookkeeping puzzle.

From monthly reconciliations to year-end clean-up, we handle the full scope so your records are accurate, compliant, and decision-ready.

№ 01

Monthly Bookkeeping

Organized, up-to-date financial records every single month. Every transaction categorized correctly so your books reflect reality — not guesswork. You'll always know what's coming in, going out, and what it means.

  • Categorization of every transaction across all accounts
  • Monthly closing within 10 business days of month-end
  • Custom chart of accounts tailored to your business
  • Secure digital document storage and audit trail
№ 02

Bank & Credit Card Reconciliation

Every bank account, credit card, and merchant account reconciled to the dollar — every month. Catch discrepancies early before they snowball into expensive surprises or audit risks.

  • Multi-account reconciliation across business and credit
  • Discrepancy investigation and resolution
  • Merchant account and processor reconciliation
  • Documented reconciliation reports for your records
№ 03

Accounts Payable & Receivable

Stay on top of every bill that's due and every dollar you're owed. We manage the cycle so vendors get paid on time, customers are invoiced promptly, and your cash flow stays healthy.

  • Vendor bill tracking, approval, and payment scheduling
  • Customer invoicing, follow-ups, and aging reports
  • Cash flow visibility across receivables and payables
  • 1099 contractor tracking and year-end preparation
№ 04

Financial Reporting

Easy-to-read financial statements that actually help you understand your business health. No jargon, no confusion — just clarity on what's working, what isn't, and where the opportunities are.

  • Monthly Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow
  • Custom KPI dashboards built around your business
  • Comparative analysis (month-over-month, year-over-year)
  • Plain-English explanations of what the numbers mean
№ 05

Clean-Up & Catch-Up

Books behind? It happens. We specialize in getting messy or neglected records back on track — no judgment, just results. Once clean, we'll keep you on a monthly system so it never happens again.

  • Multi-year catch-up bookkeeping and rebuilds
  • QuickBooks file cleanup and chart of accounts repair
  • Historical reconciliation and missing transaction recovery
  • Ongoing monthly system to prevent future backlogs
№ 06

QuickBooks Setup & Training

Whether you're starting fresh or rescuing a broken file, we handle the technical side. Proper setup now saves thousands in cleanup costs later — and gives your team the tools to stay organized.

  • QuickBooks Online setup and migration from other systems
  • Custom chart of accounts and class tracking
  • Integration with banks, payroll, and POS systems
  • Optional training for your team or in-house bookkeeper
