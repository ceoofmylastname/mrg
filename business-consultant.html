What we offer

A complete launch package.
Nothing skipped. Nothing missed.

i.

Entity Selection Guidance

LLC, S-Corp, C-Corp, partnership, or sole proprietorship? The right answer depends on your taxes, your goals, and your industry. We walk you through the trade-offs and recommend what actually fits.

ii.

Business Registration

We file the necessary documents with the state and IRS — Articles of Organization, Operating Agreements, registered agent setup, foreign qualifications. Done properly the first time.

iii.

EIN & Tax ID Application

Secure your federal tax identification number quickly and correctly. Required for banking, payroll, tax filings, and most state-level registrations.

iv.

Licenses & Permits

Industry-specific guidance on the business licenses, professional licenses, and local permits you actually need. No more guessing what your city, county, or state requires.

v.

Compliance Support

Ongoing support to ensure your business stays in good standing — annual filings, registered agent renewals, statement of information deadlines, all monitored on your behalf.

vi.

Strategic Financial Setup

The piece most founders skip and later regret: getting your bookkeeping, payroll, and tax systems organized from day one — so growth doesn't outpace your back office.
