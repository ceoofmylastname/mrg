How it works

From setup to first paycheck.

i.

Setup Call (Day 1)

We gather employee info, pay rates, schedules, benefits, and state requirements. You hand over what you have — we ask the right questions to fill in the rest.

ii.

System Configuration (Day 1–3)

QuickBooks Payroll configured. EIN and state withholding accounts confirmed. Direct deposit verified. Tax tables loaded for every state you operate in.

iii.

First Run (Day 5–7)

Test run reviewed with you. Once approved, your team gets paid on schedule — every cycle going forward.

iv.

Ongoing Management

You submit hours (or we pull them automatically). We process, file, deposit, and confirm. You get a summary every run. That's it.
